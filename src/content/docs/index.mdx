Connect your AI assistant through MCP, choose a template for your information, and ask it to save or find what you need. Yorishiro keeps that information in your own installation.

- If you have a URL and API key, [connect your assistant](installation/first-run/#4-connect-an-mcp-client).
- To run your own server, start with [Docker Compose](installation/docker-compose/), then [create your account](installation/first-run/).
- Once connected, follow [Use Yorishiro with your AI assistant](guides/using-yorishiro/).
- If something is not working, see [Troubleshooting](operations/queue-start-objectives/).
