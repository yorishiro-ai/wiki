Connect your AI assistant through MCP, choose a template for your information, and ask it to save or find what you need. Yorishiro keeps that information in your own installation.

- **New installation:** start with [Docker Compose](installation/docker-compose/), then [create your account](installation/first-run/).
- **Already connected:** follow [Use Yorishiro with your AI assistant](guides/using-yorishiro/).
- **Something is not working:** see [Troubleshooting](operations/queue-start-objectives/).
